◀ MAIN DISH RECIPES

Adobo

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp cooking oil (panggisa)
  • 1 medium onion ( sliced)
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 kilo chicken
  • 2 pcs laurel leaves
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp vinegar

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, combine all ingredients.
  2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes or until meat is tender.

Nutrition Information

Calories: ~200–250 kcal per serving

Total Fat: ~10–15g

Saturated Fat: ~3–5g

Cholesterol: ~50–100mg

Sodium: ~500–700mg

Total Carbohydrates: ~5–10g

Dietary Fiber: ~1–2g

Total Sugars: ~2–5g

Protein: ~20–25g
