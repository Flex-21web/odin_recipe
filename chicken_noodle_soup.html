◀ SOUP RECIPES

Chicken Noodle Soup

Ingredients

  • chicken drumsticks – 1,7 lb | 750 g
  • egg noodles – 4 oz | 115 g
  • carrot – 7 oz | 200 g
  • celery – 2 stalks
  • onion – 5 oz | 150 g
  • water – 2,6 qt | 2,5 l
  • salt – to taste
  • ground black pepper – to taste

Instructions

  1. 7 oz / 200 g of carrot and 2 celery stalks into small wedges, chop up 5 oz / 150 g of onion.
  2. Lay out the chicken drumsticks into a stockpot, add 6 cup | 1,5 l of water and bring to a boil, cook over medium heat for 2 minutes, drain the broth and wash the chicken.
  3. Pour again with 10 cup | 2,5 l of water, bring to a boil, skim the froth, add 1 carrot, 2 celery stalks, 1 onion, parsley sprigs, peppercorns, thyme and rosemary, cover with a lid, boil for 30 minutes over low heat.
  4. Place the chicken legs into a bowl and separate the meat from the bones and shred chicken. Take out the vegetables and strain the broth.
  5. Add the chopped onion, celery, carrot and add the chicken meat to the broth and boil for 20 minutes over low heat.
  6. Add 8 cup | 2 L of water into the second pot, bring to a boil, lay the noodles and cook until readiness over medium heat acc. package instructions.
  7. Drain the noodles and wash in cold water.
  8. Add the noodles into the soup, salt and pepper, bring to a boil and switch the heat off.
  9. Serve the noodle soup hot garnished with green onion or other greens.
